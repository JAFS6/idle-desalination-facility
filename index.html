Idle Desalination Facility

An incremental management game. Turn seawater into your next big business.

Run a plant that pumps seawater, removes the salt, bottles the freshwater and sells the finished product. Production runs automatically: you decide where to invest your earnings.

Upgrade the five stages of your production chain, balance flow rates and watch your storage tanks to resolve bottlenecks. Unlock achievements and come back to discover how much your plant has produced while you were away.
