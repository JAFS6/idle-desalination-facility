What's New

The latest improvements to your plant.

Version 0.8.0

October 19, 2026
  • Optional rewarded ads on Android let you double the water sale price for five minutes.
  • Time spent watching an ad counts towards production, without showing the offline earnings summary when you return to the game.

Version 0.7.0

October 12, 2026
  • Separate buttons to return to the game and exit while saving your progress.
  • Help explaining the game's objective and controls, available from the help button and on first launch.
  • Localized credits with links to the asset sources.

Version 0.6.0

October 7, 2026
  • Achievements for upgrading facilities, reaching new levels and earning money.
  • An achievements screen showing unlocked milestones and goals to work towards.
  • Animated achievement notifications, with unlocked milestones saved between sessions.

Version 0.5.0

September 28, 2026
  • Animated ocean water and storage tanks that visually reflect their fill levels.
  • Facility artwork with greater visual depth.
  • Compact notation makes large money totals, costs and production values easier to read.

Version 0.4.0

September 16, 2026
  • A menu with language selection, separate music and sound effect settings, game information and a game reset option.
  • Support for 18 languages, with language and sound preferences saved between sessions.
  • Production, storage and upgrade cost adjustments for more balanced progression.

Version 0.3.0

September 9, 2026
  • Offline progress when returning to the app, saved immediately to prevent duplicate rewards.
  • Improvements to touch controls, the money counter and the offline earnings summary.
