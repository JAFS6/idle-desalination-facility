# Privacy Policy for Idle Desalination Facility

Last updated: November 10, 2026.

This policy explains how your data is processed when you use **Idle Desalination Facility**, an incremental management game for Android.

## 1. Controller and contact

The controller is **Juan Antonio Fajardo Serrano**, the game's developer. For privacy enquiries or requests about your data, email **juanantoniodeveloper@gmail.com**.

## 2. Game saves and preferences

You can play without creating an account or providing contact details. The game stores your progress, achievements, temporary boosts, and language and sound preferences on your device. It also saves the game time to calculate progress while you are away and checks the system language on first launch.

These files are not sent to the developer's servers or synchronised in the cloud. Where they contain personal data, it is used only as necessary to provide the game and retain your chosen preferences, on the basis of performing that service agreement. This basis does not cover advertising.

## 3. Advertising and Google services

The game uses **Google AdMob** to offer ads you can choose to watch in exchange for temporary benefits.

We apply advertising restrictions for minors to all players without asking their age. We do not request personalised ads. This does not prevent Google and advertising providers from processing data such as IP addresses and the approximate location derived from them, interactions, diagnostics and identifiers permitted under those restrictions. This data is used to show restricted ads, measure their performance, conduct analytics and prevent fraud. The game does not request precise GPS location.

Ads may be preloaded at startup or after closing an ad, when the privacy status permits requests. Communication with these services may therefore occur before you choose to watch an ad.

We also use Google's privacy service to check that status. With the restrictive configuration applied to everyone, this service does not request player consent.

More information: [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy?hl=en) and [use of information in apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites?hl=en).

## 4. Consent and controls

Where processing requires consent or another authorisation, it must be obtained beforehand. Playing or watching an ad does not replace it. Where processing is based on consent, you can withdraw it without affecting processing lawfully carried out before withdrawal.

The game does not currently offer a control to reopen advertising preferences. For enquiries about the processing of your data, you can email the contact address given in this policy.

You can also manage the advertising identifier in Android's privacy settings. These settings do not replace the game's consent controls.

## 5. Email enquiries

If you email us, we use your address, message and the data you provide only to answer enquiries or handle requests. We do not reuse messages for advertising or disclose them to advertisers. Avoid including sensitive or unnecessary information.

General enquiries are handled on the basis of our legitimate interest in helping users and resolving issues, provided that their rights do not override that interest, particularly those of minors. Support strictly necessary to provide the game is based on performing the service agreement; requests to exercise rights are based on compliance with legal obligations. These are the applicable bases under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Ordinary enquiries and their data are deleted **12 months after resolution**. Necessary information is retained longer where there are complaints, proceedings or legal obligations. Requests to exercise rights follow their statutory time limits.

## 6. Recipients and transfers

Advertising data is disclosed to Google and the providers involved according to the AdMob configuration. The developer does not sell your game save files or preferences.

Google may process data outside your country and the European Economic Area. Its [Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy?hl=en) provides information about protection mechanisms for those transfers.

Sites or apps you open from credits or ads apply their own policies.

## 7. Retention and deletion

Your game save and preferences remain on your device until you delete them. **"Reset game"** resets progress and achievements but keeps language and sound preferences. To erase all local data, clear the app's storage through Android or uninstall it. The game does not use Android's automatic backup.

These actions do not delete data already received by Google or other advertising providers. Their retention depends on their policies, purposes and legal obligations; use their privacy channels to manage it.

## 8. Security

Local files are kept in the app's data space. According to Google, data collected by its advertising service is transmitted encrypted using TLS. No system guarantees absolute security.

## 9. Your rights

Depending on applicable law, you can request access, rectification, erasure, restriction and portability of your data, or object to processing where applicable. Email **juanantoniodeveloper@gmail.com** regarding the developer's processing.

We have no remote access to your game save. For Google's processing, also use its privacy controls and channels. You can complain to the competent authority; in Spain, the [Spanish Data Protection Agency](https://www.aepd.es/).

## 10. Minors

The game is intended for teenagers and adults. We do not ask for a date of birth or verify age.

Teenage players who are minors may also receive rewarded ads. We apply Google's advertising restrictions for minors and a general-audience advertising content filter to all players. These measures do not constitute age verification or parental authorisation.

If you are a parent or guardian with a question, you can email the contact address above.

## 11. Changes

We will update this policy when the game or its data processing practices change. The date will indicate the last update. We will provide additional notices or mechanisms where needed to inform you of changes or obtain new consent.
